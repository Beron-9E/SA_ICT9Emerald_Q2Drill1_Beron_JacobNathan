Introduction to Volleyball



Volleyball is a team sport played by two teams of six players separated by a net. The goal is to send the ball over the net and make it land on the opponent’s court. Players use skills such as serving, passing, setting, spiking, and blocking to score points. Volleyball promotes teamwork, communication, coordination, and physical fitness.

Rules
  • Each team has 6 players on the court.
  • The goal is to make the ball land inside the opponent's court.
  • Each team can touch the ball up to 3 times before sending it over the net.
  • Players cannot catch or hold the ball.
Skills
  • Serving
  • Passing
  • Setting
  • Spiking
  • Blocking
Equipment
  • Net
  • Ball
  • Posts
  • Lines
